Join us for worship as we begin our 21 Days of Prayer! Pastor Micah will kick off our new sermon series, "Seeking Him," with a message titled "Praise Him" from Psalm 145. As we begin this special time of prayer, let's come together to seek the Lord, grow in our faith, and draw closer to Him.

Welcome to Faith

Whether you are young or young at heart, exploring faith, or a lifetime follower of Christ, you are welcome here.

Faith Baptist Church is not just a collection of individuals. We are a family of believers who are following Jesus together. All of our ministries are intended to help ordinary people find a place to belong so they can experience an extraordinary life in Christ.

This church is not just a building; it’s a vibrant community where faith and fellowship intertwine. Whether it’s marriage being restored, broken families finding healing, or people overcoming life-dominating behaviors, God is using our church to produce real life change. What we believe →
