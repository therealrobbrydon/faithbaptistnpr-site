Groups

What is a Group?

Groups are bi-weekly gatherings for FBC church members to provide community, care, and content. These groups normally meet in a trained group leader’s home and are a great way to get plugged into our church community. They are where relationships are strengthened, friendships are formed, and care systems are put in place.

We hope you will make the effort to get connected to one of these groups! These Groups have a discipleship mindset which allows us to continue to grow the Kingdom of Heaven, yet stay connected to the local church. Our hope and prayer is for God’s purposes to be accomplished through this model of church ministry.

What happens in a Group?

  • Groups of all ages and demographics gather in a designated location, mostly outside of the church.

  • Groups are led by capable leaders and consist of 8-16 people.

  • Teaching content consists of a teaching video and discussion points from the pastoral staff.

  • Meetings last, a minimum of 2 hours, but may extend for more fellowship time.

How do I get involved in a Group?

  • Simply find a Group below that best fits and sign up.

  • Email Pastor Chad Houghton at pastorchad@fbcbelong.com with any questions.
